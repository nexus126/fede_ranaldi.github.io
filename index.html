About

I am a PhD researcher working at the intersection of Natural Language Processing and Neuro-Symbolic Artificial Intelligence.

My research investigates how Large Language Models acquire, represent, and use knowledge, moving beyond memorization towards generalization, reasoning, and structured understanding.

I am interested in building AI systems that combine the flexibility of neural models with the reliability, transparency, and compositionality of symbolic approaches.

Research

Structured Reasoning in LLMs

Understanding whether language models can learn formal procedures, symbolic reasoning, and algorithmic structures.

Neuro-Symbolic AI

Integrating neural architectures with formal languages, logic, knowledge representation and structured reasoning.

Adaptive and Agentic Systems

Studying how AI agents can adapt their representations, procedures and behaviours through experience.

Selected Projects

  • Structural Priming in Language Models
  • Adaptive Reasoning Agents
  • Long-term Memory and Agentic AI Systems
